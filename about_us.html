About Tabcon

Building futures.

Chancellor Angela Merkel and Vice President Dr Mahamudu Bawumia at the Germany-Ghana Business Cooperation forum, Accra, 30 August 2018. Tabcon engineers systems, infrastructure and energy solutions that move nations forward.

Who we are

Infrastructure with purpose.

Tabcon is a development consultancy focused on infrastructure, engineering, renewable energy and strategic project delivery across Africa.

We believe infrastructure is not just construction. It is economics. Energy. Water. Mobility. The architecture of national transformation.

Philosophy

Our journey

From Lesotho to the continent.

Established in 1983 in Lesotho and later, in 1988, in Ghana as Tabcon Limited, our firm has evolved into a premier consultancy driving sustainable development across the continent.
