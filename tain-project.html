Overview

A flagship renewable energy initiative.

The Tain Hybrid Hydro-Solar Energy Project is a large-scale initiative combining solar PV, hydropower and battery storage to provide reliable clean energy and regional development support.

Roadmap

Two-phase energy development plan.

PHASE 1

Solar power development

  • check_circle55 MW utility-scale solar PV plant
  • check_circleTransmission infrastructure & grid connection
  • check_circleMonitoring & control systems
PHASE 2

Hybrid hydro-solar development

  • check_circle10 MW battery energy storage system
  • check_circle5 MW hydroelectric plant on the River Tain
  • check_circleIntegrated solar + hydro dispatchability

70 MW total renewable generation capacity combining solar PV, hydropower and BESS storage, with complementary development in eco-tourism, watershed conservation, agricultural programmes and value-added processing.
