Overview

A flagship renewable energy initiative

The Tain Hybrid Hydro-Solar Energy Project is a large-scale renewable energy initiative combining solar PV, hydropower and battery storage to provide reliable clean energy and regional development support.

Two-Phase Energy Development Plan

PHASE 1

Solar Power Development

  • • 55 MW Utility-scale Solar PV Plant
  • • Transmission Infrastructure & Grid Connection
  • • Monitoring & Control Systems
PHASE 2

Hybrid Hydro-Solar Development

  • • 10 MW Battery Energy Storage System
  • • 5 MW Hydroelectric Plant on River Tain
  • • Integrated Solar + Hydro Dispatchability
