Our Heritage

Four Decades of Engineering Excellence

Founded in 1983 as TAB Consult in Lesotho, our journey began with a vision to provide specialized technical expertise to the evolving infrastructure landscape of Southern Africa.

In 1988, we expanded our horizons to West Africa, establishing Tabcon Limited in Ghana. Today, with over 40 years of experience, we stand as a beacon of reliability and innovation in multidisciplinary consulting.
