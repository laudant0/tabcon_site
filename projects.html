Tain Basin Development Project.

Live Project

55 MW Solar + 6 MW BESS | 10 MW Hydro

The Tabcon Tain Basin Project is a ~65 MW hybrid renewable energy development combining solar, hydropower, and battery storage to deliver reliable, clean electricity to the national grid.

It also supports irrigated agriculture using water from the Tain River, enabling year-round farming and boosting agribusiness. In addition, the project promotes eco-tourism through environmental conservation and the development of recreational areas around the basin.
